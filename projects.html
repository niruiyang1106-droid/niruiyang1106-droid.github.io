01 · Apr – Jul 2026

18-DoF Hexapod: Omnidirectional Locomotion & Terrain Adaptation

Project Member

A real-time control platform that combines whole-body kinematics, stable gait generation, and safety-aware motion planning.

  • Built an 18-DoF hexapod control platform in Python and CoppeliaSim for terrain-adaptive locomotion design and verification.
  • Modelled each leg as a 3-DoF kinematic chain and derived forward and inverse kinematics, including coordinate transforms and mounting geometry.
  • Implemented real-time control through the ZeroMQ Remote API and validated an omnidirectional tripod gait.
  • Designed a safety controller using Control Barrier Functions and Quadratic Programming to satisfy obstacle constraints while tracking motion targets.
  • Python
  • CoppeliaSim
  • ZeroMQ
  • Kinematics
  • CBF/QP

02 · Oct 2025 – Apr 2026

Autonomous Mobile Manipulator on Leo Rover

Project Member

An end-to-end autonomous system for exploration, target recognition, grasping, classification, placement, and recovery.

  • Integrated Leo Rover with sensing and a myCobot arm to create an autonomous mobile manipulation workflow.
  • Used MoveIt2 for motion planning and control after target perception, enabling grasp, placement, and reset behaviours.
  • Designed a finite-state machine to coordinate navigation, recognition, grasping, classification, placement, and recovery.
  • Implemented global navigation with Nav2 and A* path planning while accounting for obstacles and inaccessible map regions.
  • ROS2
  • Nav2
  • MoveIt2
  • myCobot
  • FSM
  • A*

03 · Jul 2024 – Apr 2025

Digital Twin for Power Converter Condition Monitoring

Project Member

A non-invasive digital-twin approach for monitoring the health of a DC buck converter without additional sensing circuits.

  • Analysed the converter circuit and created a four-stage progressive digital-twin model covering the power stage, sampling circuit, and closed-loop controller.
  • Validated current and voltage outputs in MATLAB/Simulink against the physical converter.
  • Applied Particle Swarm Optimisation to monitor capacitor and MOSFET health indicators and predict circuit degradation.
  • MATLAB
  • Simulink
  • PLECS
  • PSO
  • Power Electronics
